\subsection{Watch it grow}

This garden grows from seed \texttt{\ifx\florilegiumseed\empty
page\else\florilegiumseed\fi}.
Three stems rise from a root in the corner, leaves open at their
nodes as the tips pass,
and each stem ends in a flower. Then a greedy loop takes over: it
finds the largest
circle of free space within reach of a
stem\ifx\animexplain\empty\else{} (dashed)\fi,
chooses a motif that fits it, and grows a branch to it from the
nearest stem point. Big
holes come first, so flowers and lilies land early; the last small
gaps get leaflets,
buds and florets. The final frame is exactly the page that this seed
always gives.
