\subsection{One seed, one garden}

This page was grown from seed \texttt{\ifx\florilegiumseed\empty
page\else\florilegiumseed\fi}.
Nothing in the margin was placed by hand: three stems rise from a
root in the corner and
sway up the column, leaves hang at their nodes, and a greedy loop
then finds the largest
remaining hole, chooses a motif that fits it and grows a branch to it
from the nearest
stem, until only small gaps are left. The same seed always gives the
same garden; a
different seed gives a different one. Because the text on this page
ends early, the
garden also grows into the space below these lines.
